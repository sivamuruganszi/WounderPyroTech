Our Story

Lighting Up Celebrations Since Day One

Wonder Pyro Tech is a wholesale fireworks supplier based in Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu — India's fireworks capital — bringing quality crackers and sky displays to families and retailers across the region at honest wholesale prices.

Who we are

Built on a family tradition of craftsmanship

Wonder Pyro Tech began as a small family-run stall supplying sparklers and one-sound crackers to neighbouring towns during Diwali season. Over the years, steady demand and word-of-mouth trust from retailers turned that stall into a full wholesale operation — today offering a catalog of 75+ products spanning sparklers, flower pots, ground chakkars, novelties, and multi-inch fancy sky-display shells.

We're still run the same way we started: directly from Sivakasi's manufacturing belt, with no unnecessary middlemen, so our partners get factory-fresh stock at genuine wholesale rates — not inflated retail markups.

Safety & Compliance

Fireworks are a serious product, and we treat them that way. Here's what we commit to on every order:

  • Stock sourced only from licensed, compliant fireworks manufacturers in Sivakasi.
  • Products packed and stored per standard explosive-safety handling guidelines.
  • Sale intended for licensed dealers, retailers, and adults, in line with local regulations on fireworks purchase and use.
  • Clear actual vs. discount pricing on every item — no hidden charges at checkout.
  • Orders confirmed personally over phone/WhatsApp before dispatch, so nothing ships without your sign-off.
